# Medium biography (~120 words)

I'm a software engineer and application architect focused on developer experience, source generation, delivery automation, and the engineering foundations that help teams move quickly without sacrificing quality.

I created Purview-Dev to make software development easier, faster, and more efficient. Too much engineering time is lost to repetitive setup, inconsistent tooling, fragile conventions, and processes teams must continually rebuild. Purview turns those recurring problems into focused, reusable, and well-documented open-source tools — from compile-time telemetry generation and aggregate-based event sourcing to strongly typed validation, source-generator infrastructure, and shared build pipelines.

The goal is not to hide engineering decisions or replace good judgement. It is to remove unnecessary friction so developers can spend more time solving the problems that matter.
